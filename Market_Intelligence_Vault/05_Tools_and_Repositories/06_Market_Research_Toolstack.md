# Extended Market Research Toolstack

This document maps out the 10 additional platforms requested, their pricing plans (as of mid-2026), core use cases, and exactly how they plug into the Market Intelligence Engine to find and validate new ideas.

## 1. AI Co-Founders & Market Research Agents

### [Frederick.ai](https://app.frederick.ai/projects/new)
- **What it is:** An "AI cofounder" platform that provides AI agent teammates (coding agents, browser agents, background agents) to build software and run operations.
- **Latest Pricing (2026):** 
  - **Free Plan:** Basic access, ~15 AI credits, 1GB storage (good for hobbyists).
  - **Plus/Pro Plans:** Scaling up (typically $16-$32/mo) for full features, more credits, custom domains, and autonomous background tasks.
- **Relevance for Us:** Can be used in **Layer 2 (Pain Point Mining)** and **Layer 3 (Gap Synthesis)**. Its background agents can autonomously scrape competitors or build the automated landing page for the Fake Door test.

### [Yabble](http://yabble.com/)
- **What it is:** An AI-powered market research and insights platform.
- **Latest Pricing (2026):** 
  - **Starter:** ~$800/month (core tools and limited virtual personas).
  - **Professional:** ~$1,500/month (unlimited personas, API access).
  - **Enterprise:** Custom pricing.
- **Relevance for Us:** Extremely powerful for **Idea Validation**. Instead of waiting for real users, you can use Yabble's *Virtual Audiences* to simulate your target market and test your startup idea instantly against AI personas before running ads.

## 2. Macro Market & Industry Data

### [Statista](https://www.statista.com/)
- **What it is:** A global portal for statistics, consumer survey results, and industry studies.
- **Latest Pricing (2026):** 
  - **Basic:** Free (very limited).
  - **Starter:** ~$199/month (billed annually).
  - **Personal:** ~$649/month (premium reports).
  - **Professional:** ~$1,299/month (team access).
- **Relevance for Us:** Used in **Layer 1 (Macro Scan)**. Before mining Reddit, use Statista to confirm the Total Addressable Market (TAM) is growing and to find high-level macro trends in Fintech or SaaS.

### [IBEF (India Brand Equity Foundation)](https://www.ibef.org/industry)
- **What it is:** A trust established by the Department of Commerce, Ministry of Commerce and Industry, Government of India. It provides macroeconomic data and industry reports for the Indian market.
- **Latest Pricing:** Free public access to industry reports.
- **Relevance for Us:** If you are targeting the Indian market or offshoring, IBEF provides free, incredibly detailed PDF reports on sector growth (e.g., Indian SaaS, IT Infra) to validate your Macro Scan.

## 3. Search Volume & SEO Validation Tools

### [SEMrush](https://www.semrush.com/)
- **What it is:** A premium all-in-one SEO, keyword research, and competitor analysis suite.
- **Latest Pricing (2026):** 
  - **SEO Classic Plans:** Pro ($139.95/mo), Guru ($249.95/mo), Business ($499.95/mo).
  - **Semrush One (AI tools included):** Starter ($199/mo), Pro+ ($299/mo).
- **Relevance for Us:** The ultimate validation tool. Use it to reverse-engineer exactly what keywords your competitors are bidding on, how much traffic they get, and to validate if people are actively searching for the solution you synthesized in Layer 3.

### [Ubersuggest (Neil Patel)](https://app.neilpatel.com/en/new-dashboard)
- **What it is:** A more affordable, user-friendly keyword research and SEO tool.
- **Latest Pricing (2026):** 
  - **Individual:** ~$12/mo (or ~$120 Lifetime).
  - **Business:** ~$20/mo (or ~$200 Lifetime).
  - **Enterprise:** ~$40/mo (or ~$400 Lifetime).
- **Relevance for Us:** A budget-friendly alternative to SEMrush. Fits perfectly into the **$0-$500 Guardrail budget**. Use it to check search volume for the exact "Frustration Phrases" (e.g., "how to fix X") you found on Reddit.

### [Google Keyword Planner](https://business.google.com/in/ad-tools/keyword-planner/)
- **What it is:** Google's native tool for advertisers to discover new keywords and see estimated search volumes and costs to target them.
- **Latest Pricing:** Free (requires a Google Ads account).
- **Relevance for Us:** Crucial for the **Fake Door Test**. Before spending your $500 on ads, use Keyword Planner to forecast exactly how many clicks you can afford and which keywords have the highest commercial intent.

### [Google Trends](https://trends.google.com/trends/)
- **What it is:** Analyzes the popularity of top search queries in Google Search across various regions and languages.
- **Latest Pricing:** 100% Free.
- **Relevance for Us:** Used in **Layer 1 (Macro Scan)**. As Steph France noted, use Trends to ensure your market is a steady, growing *Topic* based on human needs, rather than a volatile fad.

## 4. Monitoring & Analytics

### [Google Analytics 360](https://marketingplatform.google.com/about/analytics-360/)
- **What it is:** The enterprise-level version of Google Analytics, offering advanced modeling, unsampled reports, and BigQuery integration.
- **Latest Pricing:** Enterprise custom pricing (historically starts at $150,000/year, though GA4 pricing scales by event volume). Standard Google Analytics is free.
- **Relevance for Us:** While GA360 is overkill for a lean startup, standard Google Analytics is mandatory for the **Fake Door Test**. You use it to track the exact conversion rate of users who hit your landing page and click the "Waitlist" button.

### [Google Alerts](https://www.google.co.in/alerts)
- **What it is:** A content change detection and notification service.
- **Latest Pricing:** 100% Free.
- **Relevance for Us:** The ultimate passive listening tool. Set up alerts for your competitors' names, or phrases like "new SaaS for [niche]" to get emails whenever they are mentioned on the web. It automates a portion of your ongoing Market Intelligence gathering.
