# Validation Strategy & The $500 Guardrail

Based on the initial prompt and Steph France's transcript, this is the exact protocol to prevent wasting time and money on unvalidated ideas.

## The Rule
**Do not build the complex backend yet.** With a $0-$500 budget and 15-25 hrs/week, you must simulate the business before writing a single line of production code.

## 1. Automated Landing Page Concept
- Use the Gap Synthesis output (from Claude) or the Landing Page Prompt Creator.
- Feed this prompt into an AI UI generator like **Lovable** or wireframe it rapidly in **Framer** (as seen in the Gummy Search video).
- Ensure the copy literally tells the customer their pain point in their own language (extracted directly from Reddit quotes).

## 2. The "Fake Door" Test (Quiz / Waitlist)
- Do not have a working product behind the "Sign Up" button.
- Instead, trigger a pop-up or redirect to a survey/quiz.
- *Steph France's exact message strategy:* "Thank you for being here. The app is in development right now. We are a small team and would love to have your take on your current problem. Would you mind answering a few questions?"
- *Call to Action:* "Do you want us to let you know when the app is out there?" (Waitlist capture).

## 3. Traffic Generation
- **Paid (The $500 Guardrail):** Use your budget for small, hyper-targeted ads (Google / LinkedIn) driving traffic to the landing page.
- **Organic (Gummy Search strategy):** Use the "Top Content" format analysis to post highly relevant stories/memes in the targeted subreddits to drive organic traffic.

## 4. The Signal to Build
- If people click "Sign Up" or fill out the quiz when there is no product, you have a validated gap.
- *Threshold:* Grow the list. Decide on a metric for yourself (e.g., "when I have 100 people on my waiting list, I'm building the app as fast as I can"). 
- Once you hit that threshold, build the MVP and launch it to that exact list via email.
