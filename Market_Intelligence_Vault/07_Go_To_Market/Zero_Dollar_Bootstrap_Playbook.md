## 2. The $0 Organic Distribution Engine (Zero Ads)

You do **NOT** need Google Ads or paid marketing. Developers respond 10x better to authentic developer-to-developer posts than sponsored ads:

### 1. Hacker News "Show HN" ($0)
* **Post Time:** Tuesday or Wednesday between 6:00 AM – 8:00 AM PT (Peak traffic window).
* **Format:** Transparent, developer-focused, no marketing fluff.
* **Expected Result:** 500 – 2,500 unique developer visitors in 24 hours.

### 2. Reddit Value-First Case Studies ($0)
* **Subreddits:** `r/webdev`, `r/devops`, `r/SaaS`, `r/nextjs`, `r/node`.
* **Strategy:** Share the architectural breakdown: *"How we designed a zero-latency Dead-Letter Queue proxy using Cloudflare Workers and Upstash Redis."*
* Include a GitHub demo repo link + your landing page link.
* **Expected Result:** 30–80 high-intent early adopter signups.

### 3. Open-Source GitHub Wedge ($0)
* Open-source the lightweight CLI (`webhookguard-cli`) on GitHub.
* Submit it to repositories like `awesome-selfhosted`, `awesome-developer-tools`, and `producthunt`.
* Devs star and share developer tooling organically.

### 4. Direct 1-on-1 Founder Outreach ($0)
* Search Twitter/X or Reddit for phrases like *"Stripe webhook failed"*, *"ngrok alternative webhook"*, or *"invoice webhook broken"*.
* Reply with a friendly, direct message offering your free beta tool.
