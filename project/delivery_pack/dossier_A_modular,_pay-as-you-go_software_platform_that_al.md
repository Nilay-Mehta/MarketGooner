# AdaptivOS: Product Dossier

**Product Name:** AdaptivOS

---

## 1) Executive Summary

AdaptivOS is a pioneering modular SaaS platform engineered to redefine how small to medium-sized businesses (SMBs) and startups acquire and utilize essential business software. In an era dominated by expensive, all-inclusive SaaS bundles and restrictive feature-gating, AdaptivOS offers a refreshing alternative. Our platform empowers users to construct a bespoke toolset from a comprehensive, ever-growing library of core business functionalities (e.g., CRM, invoicing, project management).

The core innovation lies in our pay-as-you-go model, which, combined with a robust free tier, ensures businesses only invest in the specific features they actively need. This eliminates the financial burden of unused software, fosters unparalleled scalability, and provides true vendor flexibility. AdaptivOS directly addresses the pain points of cost-conscious SMBs and agile startups seeking flexible, integrated solutions without the overhead and complexity of enterprise-level pricing. We are building the adaptable operating system for the modern, lean business.

---

## 2) Core Features

AdaptivOS is built on a foundation of modularity, flexibility, and user control. Each feature is designed as an independent, yet seamlessly integrated, module that users can activate or deactivate on demand.

*   **Modular Feature Library:** A curated and expanding collection of essential business tools available for selection:
    *   **CRM Lite:** Contact management, lead tracking, basic sales pipeline.
    *   **Invoicing & Billing:** Professional invoice generation, payment tracking, expense logging.
    *   **Project & Task Management:** Task assignment, progress tracking, team collaboration, Kanban boards.
    *   **Reporting & Analytics (Basic):** Customizable dashboards, key performance indicators (KPIs) for activated modules.
    *   **Document Management:** Secure storage, sharing, and version control for business documents.
    *   **Basic HR:** Employee directory, time-off requests, simple attendance tracking.
    *   **Lightweight Marketing Automation:** Email campaign builder, contact segmentation.
