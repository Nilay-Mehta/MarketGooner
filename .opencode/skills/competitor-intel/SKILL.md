# Competitor Intelligence & Unbundling Skill

## 1. Incumbent Mapping
Identify top 3-5 existing platforms serving the domain and categorize them:
- **Legacy Monoliths:** (e.g. Salesforce, SAP, Oracle) - High churn risk, complex, slow.
- **Modern Giants:** (e.g. Datadog, Stripe, HubSpot) - Feature-bloated, expensive enterprise pricing.
- **Indie Tools / Point Solutions:** Low market awareness, missing key integrations.

## 2. Review Mining & Flaw Extraction
Scan 1-star, 2-star, and 3-star reviews for:
- "The pricing jump after the free tier is insane."
- "Takes 3 months to onboard and requires a full-time admin."
- "We only use 5% of the features but pay for 100%."

## 3. The Unbundling Playbook
Identify the **single feature** that 80% of users actually care about:
- Strip away all the enterprise bloat.
- Package that single feature into a standalone tool that takes 60 seconds to set up.
- Price at 20% of the monolithic incumbent's cost ($49-$149/mo).
